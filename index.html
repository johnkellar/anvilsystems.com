Engagement Models

Three ways to
work together

Engagements fall into three categories. Leadership engagements are ongoing relationships, providing presence, judgment, and accountability. Delivery engagements are hands-on, building and maintaining your platform. Assessment engagements are scoped, with a defined deliverable and a clear endpoint.

Leadership Ongoing · Monthly Retainer
01

Fractional CTO

Ongoing technology leadership on a part-time basis. Embedded inside your team working on the problems that matter to your business. Best for companies that need ongoing technology leadership without a full-time CTO.

20, 40, or 80 hours per month
3 Month Minimum
02

Interim CTO

Full-time technology leadership providing coverage and continuity during a CTO transition. Focused on stabilizing the team, hiring, and onboarding a new CTO, then handing off smoothly. Optional transition to Strategic Advisor after handoff.

Full-time or near full-time
6 Month Minimum

Strategic Advisor

Common Questions

Before you reach out

What's the difference between a fractional CTO and a consultant? +
A consultant delivers a recommendation and leaves. A fractional CTO operates inside your team, attending standups, making architecture calls, mentoring your engineers, and being accountable for outcomes over time. The relationship is ongoing, not transactional.
How do I know which engagement is right for me? +
If you need ongoing technology leadership, start with Fractional CTO. If your CTO just left and you need coverage now, that's Interim CTO. If you have a technical team but need outside perspective on key decisions, Strategic Advisor. If you need something built or kept running, look at Delivery. If you need an independent read on where things stand, look at Assessment. Not sure? Schedule a discovery call so we can figure it out together.
How long do engagements last? +
Leadership and Delivery engagements start with a 3 or 6 month minimum, depending on the offering. Most clients continue beyond the initial term. Assessment engagements have defined timelines agreed upfront. The goal is always to build internal capability, not to create dependency.
Why a retainer instead of time and materials? +

In a word, predictability.

A retainer reserves hours. The number is agreed upfront, so your budget is predictable and there are no surprise invoices.

A retainer reserves priority. Your hours are carved out in advance, so the work is scheduled rather than queued.

A retainer reserves capacity. When nothing is urgent, that time goes to building context on your systems, your constraints, and your risks, which makes every later hour more effective.

If you only need a few hours of help once, we will say so on the discovery call.

Do you work with pre-revenue startups? +
Yes, but only when there's a real technical challenge that justifies the engagement. If you're pre-revenue and need help with architecture decisions, team building, compliance groundwork, or fundraising preparation, a fractional CTO can be the most cost-effective path. If you just need code written, you need an engineer. We can help you find and hire the right one.
